04. Featured Projects

🚦 FLAGSHIP · EDGE AI · 100% ON-DEVICE · PRIVACY-FIRST

Driving CivicSense: Edge AI Perception for Road Safety

On-device AI for intersection discipline, lane courtesy, road hazard alerts & cooperative safety. Built on 50,000 miles of real driving footage + LiDAR, deployed as YOLOv8/v11 INT8 → TensorRT at ~12 ms on the NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano Super NX, streamed to a Raspberry Pi Zero 2W UDP server. Zero video leaves the car.

Includes a published paper on Intersection Blockage Prediction, an ISO 26262 safety study, and an edge VLM serving stack (vLLM + TensorRT-LLM in Docker on the Jetson).
